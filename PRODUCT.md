# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers looking for a Full Stack Developer. They arrive at this page to quickly assess who João Victor Diniz is, what he does, and how to reach him. Their context: brief, focused, scanning for competence signals and contact paths. The job to be done: "Can I see this person's professional presence and get in touch in under 30 seconds?"

in pt-BR

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and link-in-bio page that serves as João Victor Diniz's professional home on the web. It exists to make a warm, confident first impression that communicates skill and approachability simultaneously. Success looks like: a visitor lands, immediately understands who João is, feels invited to connect, and clicks through to a social profile or sends an email.

## Brand Personality

Warm, Approachable, Skilled.

Warm: the page feels like a handshake, not a billboard. Friendly tones, readable type, comfortable spacing.
Approachable: nothing intimidating or exclusive. Easy to read, easy to click, easy to reach out.
Skilled: warmth doesn't mean casual. Every detail is considered, the code is clean, and the visual choices signal competence.

## Anti-references

- Generic link-tree pages: rows of buttons on a dark background with no personality
- Overly dark/cyberpunk aesthetics: neon glows, matrix vibes, tech-for-tech's-sake
- Corporate/enterprise stiffness: formal, cold, suit-and-tie energy
- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient hero sections, metric blocks, card grids with icons

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't list**: Lead with presence and personality, not a bullet list of skills. A face, a title, and a clear path forward beats a resume wall.
2. **Warmth through craft**: Approachability comes from considered details, comfortable spacing, and inviting color, not from being casual or informal.
3. **Respect the scan**: Recruiters are fast. Every element should either answer a question or invite the next click. Nothing decorative that doesn't earn its place.
4. **Confidence in restraint**: Skilled means knowing what to leave out. The page is a focused entry point, not a portfolio gallery. One strong impression over many weak ones.
5. **Bridge, not billboard**: This page connects João to people. Every interaction should feel like an open door: obvious, easy, and welcoming.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Sufficient contrast ratios for all text, keyboard-navigable links, meaningful alt text on the profile image, and semantic HTML throughout. Consider reduced motion for any interactive elements.
